Signs Parents Often Notice

  • Mouth breathing or persistent blocked nose
  • Snoring or restless sleep
  • Recurring colds, ear concerns, or throat infections
  • Nasal voice or daytime tiredness
  • Difficulty concentrating or missed school days

Child-Centered Assessment

We review breathing and sleep patterns, infection history, allergies, growth, ENT findings, hearing concerns, current medicines, and the effect on family life.

Adenoid size alone does not determine care. Examination by a pediatrician or ENT specialist, hearing evaluation, or sleep assessment may be recommended.

Struggling to breathe, blue lips, pauses in breathing, severe drowsiness, dehydration, or rapidly worsening illness needs urgent pediatric care. The program does not replace ENT, emergency, or surgical care.
