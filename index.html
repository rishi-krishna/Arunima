How Our Assessment-to-Program Process Works

From the first conversation to a structured program—three steps, each with a clear purpose.

1

Book Your Assessment

Spend 60 minutes one-to-one with Arunima Mustyala. We review your complete case history, earlier treatment, and what homeopathy may realistically offer.

The ₹999 assessment fee is credited in full if you enroll.

2

Get a Clear Recommendation

If homeopathy is a good fit, you receive a defined protocol, timeline, milestones, and individualized medicines—not a standard formula.

If another specialist would serve you better, we will say so and recommend referral.

3

Begin Your Program

Your medicines are dispatched, progress tracking begins, and clinic-hour WhatsApp support is available for questions along the way.

Many suitable cases notice measurable changes within 6–10 weeks; individual results vary.

About Our Clinic 3 questions

Longer assessments, individualized medicines, defined program milestones, and transparent referral guidance form the core of our approach.
Care is organized into time-bound protocols with documented outcomes, scheduled reviews, and a clear conversation about fit before enrollment.
Core areas include chronic skin concerns, PCOD and fibroid support, pediatric adenoids, allergies, hair loss, and thyroid-related wellbeing.

Pricing & Payment 3 questions

Six-month programs begin at ₹26,000 and nine-month FibroCare begins at ₹35,000. Your recommendation and exact fee are discussed after assessment.
Yes. If you enroll in a recommended structured program, the ₹999 assessment fee is credited in full.
Flexible payment options are available. The clinic will explain applicable schedules before you enroll.

Program Details 3 questions

Personalized medicines, scheduled consultations, milestone tracking, relevant lifestyle guidance, and clinic-hour WhatsApp support are included.
The programs are supportive homeopathic care. They do not replace urgent, surgical, or specialist treatment when clinically indicated.
You do not need to choose alone. The assessment determines whether a protocol fits your concern and which level of support is appropriate.

Timeline & Results 2 questions

Many suitable cases notice a measurable difference within 6–10 weeks, but severity, constitution, and adherence all affect timing.
Progress is reviewed against agreed milestones. If the expected direction is not seen, the plan is reassessed and referral is discussed where appropriate.

Support & Safety 2 questions

Enrolled patients may ask brief program questions during clinic hours. WhatsApp is not monitored as an emergency service.
Suitability must be assessed individually. Pregnancy, childhood illness, severe symptoms, and existing medication require full disclosure and coordination with relevant clinicians.
