Concerns We Review

  • Irregular, delayed, or absent periods
  • Heavy or prolonged bleeding
  • Pelvic pressure, cramps, or bloating
  • Acne, unwanted hair growth, or weight changes
  • Fatigue and cycle-related mood changes

Your Assessment

We review cycle history, scans and laboratory reports, pain and bleeding patterns, metabolic health, fertility goals, medication, sleep, stress, and nutrition.

Fibroids and PCOD require appropriate medical diagnosis. We coordinate with gynecology and never advise delaying indicated investigations, surgery, or urgent care.

A Nine-Month, Review-Led Timeline
