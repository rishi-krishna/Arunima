Concerns We Review

  • Sudden or prolonged diffuse shedding
  • Patchy hair loss or widening part
  • Post-illness or post-stress shedding
  • Dandruff, itching, or scalp sensitivity
  • Hair changes linked with PCOD or thyroid concerns

Assessment Before Treatment

We document onset, distribution, daily shedding, scalp changes, diet, weight change, stress, recent illness, childbirth, medication, and family pattern.

Blood tests or dermatology review may be needed to evaluate anemia, thyroid function, nutrient deficiency, infection, scarring alopecia, or hormonal causes.

How Progress Is Reviewed

1

Month 1 · Baseline

Standardized photographs, shedding estimate, scalp review, relevant tests, and care plan.

2

Months 2–3 · Shedding

Review hair fall, breakage, scalp comfort, nutrition, adherence, and any new warning signs.

3

Months 4–6 · Density

Compare photographs, part width, new growth, scalp health, and longer-term next steps.
