Understanding PCOS and Homeopathy

PCOS can affect cycles, skin, hair, fertility, and long-term metabolic health. Treatment works best when the goals and diagnosis are clear.

PCOS is more than irregular periods

Possible features include infrequent ovulation, acne, unwanted hair growth, scalp hair thinning, difficulty conceiving, and insulin resistance. Not every patient has every feature.

Diagnosis requires careful evaluation

Clinicians consider cycle and androgen signs, sometimes ovarian appearance, and exclusion of conditions such as thyroid disorders or high prolactin. An ultrasound alone does not establish PCOS.

Treatment follows your priorities

Cycle protection, acne or hair symptoms, fertility, metabolic risk, sleep, and emotional wellbeing may call for different combinations of lifestyle support and prescribed treatment.

Where supportive homeopathy may fit

A whole-case assessment may be added for symptom and wellbeing support while gynecological and metabolic care continues. It should not replace indicated contraception, fertility treatment, diabetes prevention, or investigation.

Track meaningful outcomes

Record cycle length, bleeding, pain, acne, hair changes, energy, and laboratory measures advised by your doctor. Review the plan when there is no useful change.

Medical safety note

Very heavy bleeding, fainting, severe pelvic pain, pregnancy-related pain or bleeding, or symptoms of very high blood sugar needs prompt medical care. Seek help for severe low mood or thoughts of self-harm.
