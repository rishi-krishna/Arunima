1. Scope

This policy applies to personal information handled by Dr. Arunima Mustyala through appointments, consultations, telephone or online communication, and this website. It does not govern independent hospitals, laboratories, payment providers, or platforms with their own policies.

2. Information we collect

Depending on your interaction, information may include name, contact details, age, appointment preferences, health history, symptoms, prescriptions, reports, photographs, consultation notes, payment records, consent choices, and communications. Website systems may process basic device, browser, and cookie information.

Please do not send emergency information or unnecessary sensitive data through ordinary email, public messaging, or demonstration forms.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to schedule and deliver care, maintain clinical and billing records, communicate reminders and follow-up, meet professional or legal obligations, protect patients and the clinic, improve services, and respond to requests. Marketing messages are sent only where a lawful basis or consent applies, and you may opt out.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared with authorized clinic staff, technology or payment providers acting under appropriate terms, or another healthcare professional with consent or when safety or law requires it. The clinic does not sell patient health information. If data is processed outside your region, reasonable contractual and technical safeguards are sought.

5. Security and retention

Administrative, physical, and technical measures are used in proportion to the information involved. No transmission or storage system is completely secure. Records are retained for periods required by professional, clinical, tax, dispute, and legal obligations, then securely deleted or anonymized where practical.

6. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and clinical-record duties, you may ask to access or correct information, withdraw optional consent, object to certain uses, or request deletion or restriction. Some records cannot be removed when retention is legally or clinically required. Identity verification may be requested.

7. Cookies and website data

The website may use essential local storage for preferences such as theme and cookie acknowledgement. Any optional analytics or marketing technology should be enabled only after the appropriate consent mechanism. Browser controls can remove stored data, though some features may reset.

8. Contact and complaints

Questions or privacy requests may be submitted through the consultation page.

We may update this policy when practices or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
