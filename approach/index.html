Assessment to follow-up

A Clear, Individualized Approach

Care begins with a complete case, moves through a written recommendation, and continues with planned reviews.

The care pathway

What to expect

1. Detailed assessment

A 60-minute consultation considers symptoms, timeline, triggers, prior treatment, investigations, lifestyle, and your priorities.

2. Clear recommendation

You receive an honest suitability discussion, expected review rhythm, program options, and circumstances that require referral.

3. Measured follow-up

Reviews compare agreed markers such as symptom frequency, intensity, sleep, daily function, photographs, or relevant reports.

Individualized, not improvised

How decisions are made

  • One-to-one case analysis rather than a condition-only template
  • A documented baseline and practical goals
  • Planned follow-ups with adjustments where appropriate
  • Coordination with investigations and conventional care
  • Transparent fees, inclusions, and program boundaries

Safety comes first

Homeopathic care is not a substitute for emergency treatment, surgery, essential medication, or specialist assessment. Never stop prescribed medication without the advice of the prescribing clinician.

Red-flag symptoms and conditions outside scope are referred promptly.

Progress without promises

Review what matters

Every case is different. Timelines and response cannot be guaranteed. The purpose of review is to identify meaningful change, lack of progress, adverse developments, and the safest next step.

Symptoms

Frequency, severity, duration, and flare patterns.

Function

Sleep, school, work, movement, and daily comfort.

Objective records

Photographs, reports, or clinician-advised tests where relevant.

Patient goals

Progress against priorities agreed at assessment.
