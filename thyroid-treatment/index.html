Concerns We Review

Assessment with Medical Context

We review diagnosis, TSH and thyroid hormone results, antibodies where relevant, medication and adherence, symptoms, nutrition, sleep, stress, cycle history, and other health conditions.

Thyroid hormone replacement and antithyroid medicines must never be stopped or adjusted without the prescribing physician. Homeopathic care does not replace laboratory monitoring.

Urgent Symptoms Need Medical Care

Chest pain, severe breathlessness, fainting, confusion, extreme drowsiness, a very fast or slow heartbeat, or thyroid symptoms during pregnancy need prompt medical assessment. Do not rely on supportive care alone.
